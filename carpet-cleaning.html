Kansas City's Premier Carpet Cleaning Service

Your carpet takes on more than you think — foot traffic, pet dander, spilled drinks, dust mites, and allergens all work their way deep into the fibers over time. At KC Clean Carpet, we use professional-grade hot water extraction equipment and eco-friendly, non-toxic cleaning solutions to flush out what regular vacuuming simply can't reach.

Whether you have a single room that needs attention or a full home or commercial space, our trained technicians deliver consistent, heroic results every time. We treat your home with respect, protect your baseboards and furniture, and leave your carpets fresh, clean, and fast-drying.

Our Carpet Cleaning Process

  • Pre-inspection — We assess fiber type, soiling level, and any problem areas before we begin.
  • Pre-treatment — A specialized pre-spray is applied to break down embedded dirt and stains.
  • Hot water extraction — High-pressure hot water is injected deep into fibers and extracted along with dirt, allergens, and residue.
  • Spot treatment — Stubborn stains get targeted attention with professional-grade spotting agents.
  • Post-grooming — Carpet fibers are groomed to restore pile direction and speed drying.
  • Final inspection — We walk through with you to make sure you're completely satisfied before we leave.

Why Hot Water Extraction?

Hot water extraction (also known as steam cleaning) is the method most recommended by carpet manufacturers and the IICRC (Institute of Inspection Cleaning and Restoration Certification). It removes more dirt, bacteria, and allergens than dry cleaning or shampooing, and leaves no soapy residue behind that would attract future soiling.

We Clean All Carpet Types

  • Berber & loop pile carpet
  • Cut pile, plush, and frieze carpets
  • Wool and natural fiber carpets
  • Commercial and Berber tiles
  • Area rugs (in-home service available)
  • Stairs and hallways

Pet Stain & Odor Removal

Pet accidents are one of the most challenging carpet problems — urine can soak through the carpet into the padding and subfloor if not properly treated. Our enzyme-based treatments break down the odor-causing compounds at a molecular level, eliminating the smell rather than masking it. We can also apply a protective treatment to help prevent future odor absorption.
