Professional Hardwood Floor Cleaning in Kansas City

Hardwood floors are one of the most beautiful — and most vulnerable — features in your home. Over time, everyday foot traffic grinds fine particles of grit and dirt into the finish, dulling the surface and causing microscopic scratches. Standard mopping pushes dirty water into the seams and can cause warping, cupping, and finish damage if not done correctly.

At KC Clean Carpet, we use specialized low-moisture hardwood cleaning systems designed specifically to clean and refresh wood floors safely, without over-wetting the surface or leaving behind residue that attracts more dirt.

Our Hardwood Floor Cleaning Process

  • Dry soil removal — We begin with thorough dry extraction to remove loose grit and debris before any liquid is applied.
  • pH-neutral cleaning solution — A wood-safe, pH-neutral cleaner is applied in controlled amounts to break down embedded grime and oils.
  • Controlled scrubbing — Soft-bristle oscillating pads gently agitate the floor surface without scratching the finish.
  • Immediate extraction — Cleaning solution and loosened soils are immediately extracted, preventing moisture absorption into the wood.
  • Buff & dry — The floor is buffed dry, leaving a clean, streak-free surface that's ready to walk on within minutes.

Hardwood Floors We Service

  • Solid hardwood (oak, maple, walnut, cherry, and more)
  • Engineered hardwood
  • Prefinished and site-finished floors
  • Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and laminate floors
  • Bamboo and cork flooring

Signs Your Hardwood Floors Need Professional Cleaning

  • Surface looks dull or hazy even after regular mopping
  • Sticky or grimy feel underfoot
  • Build-up of cleaning product residue
  • Visible scuff marks and light surface scratches
  • Discoloration near high-traffic areas

Maintenance Tips Between Cleanings

To protect your investment between professional cleanings, we recommend dry-mopping or vacuuming with a soft-bristle floor attachment weekly, using furniture pads under all chair and table legs, placing entry mats at all exterior doors, and avoiding steam mops or excessive water. Ask our technicians about the right maintenance products for your specific floor finish.
