Our Commercial Cleaning Process

Every commercial job is scoped specifically for the facility. We don't apply a one-size-fits-all residential approach to commercial spaces — the equipment, technique, and logistics are purpose-built for the demands of business environments.

Step 1 — Facility Walk-Through & Assessment

Before any cleaning begins, we walk the entire space with you to assess carpet condition, identify high-traffic zones, spot problem areas (stains, wear patterns, high-soil corridors), and discuss any scheduling constraints. You'll receive a clear scope of work and pricing before we start.

Step 2 — Pre-Treatment

A commercial-grade pre-spray is applied to break down embedded soil, grease, and staining. High-traffic areas like entryways, hallways, and paths between workstations receive concentrated attention. Spot treatments are applied to individual stains as needed.

Step 3 — Truck-Mount Hot Water Extraction

Our truck-mounted equipment delivers significantly more heat, pressure, and suction than portable units — critical for commercial jobs where deep soil extraction and fast dry times are essential. Hot water is injected into the carpet fibers at high pressure and immediately extracted along with dirt, allergens, bacteria, and residue.

Step 4 — High-Traffic Zone Detail Work

Entryways, hallways, elevator landings, and paths to restrooms take the most abuse. These zones receive additional passes and, when needed, rotary agitation to ensure deep-seated soil is fully extracted from the fibers.

Step 5 — Speed Drying

Commercial air movers are placed strategically throughout the cleaned area to accelerate drying. Most spaces are dry and ready for foot traffic within 2–4 hours. For overnight jobs, carpet is typically fully dry before your staff arrives the next morning.

Step 6 — Final Inspection & Walk-Through

We walk the space with your facility manager or point of contact to verify that every area meets expectations. Any remaining concerns are addressed on the spot before we pack up.

After-hours & weekend scheduling available. Most of our commercial jobs are completed in the evening, overnight, or on weekends — so there is zero disruption to your daily business operations. Your staff and customers never need to see us or step around equipment.

Recurring Maintenance Programs

One-time deep cleans restore appearance, but regular professional maintenance is what keeps commercial carpet looking sharp and extends its lifespan. Our maintenance programs are designed for businesses that need their carpet to consistently look its best.

  • Monthly service — Ideal for high-traffic environments: hotels, medical offices, retail stores, and buildings with 100+ daily visitors.
  • Bi-monthly service — The most popular option for standard offices, churches, and community centers with moderate foot traffic.
  • Quarterly service — Suitable for low-traffic professional offices, conference spaces, and executive areas that see lighter daily use.

All recurring plans include priority scheduling, locked-in pricing, and a dedicated point of contact who knows your facility. We'll also provide a customized maintenance checklist so your janitorial staff knows how to best maintain the carpet between our visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you clean carpets after business hours?

Yes. We offer after-hours and weekend scheduling specifically for commercial clients. Most of our office and retail jobs are completed in the evening or overnight so there is zero disruption to your business operations.

How often should commercial carpets be professionally cleaned?

Most commercial spaces benefit from professional hot water extraction every 3–6 months, depending on foot traffic. High-traffic environments like hotels, medical offices, and retail stores may need monthly or bi-monthly service. We can set up a recurring maintenance schedule tailored to your facility.

Can you clean large commercial spaces in one visit?

Yes. Our commercial-grade truck-mount equipment is designed for large-scale jobs. We can clean thousands of square feet in a single session. For very large facilities, we can also break the job into zones and clean over multiple visits to minimize disruption.

Do you offer recurring commercial cleaning contracts?

Yes. We offer monthly, bi-monthly, and quarterly maintenance plans at discounted rates. Recurring contracts include priority scheduling, consistent pricing, and a dedicated account manager who knows your facility.

How long does it take for commercial carpet to dry after cleaning?

With our high-powered extraction equipment and commercial air movers, most carpets are dry and ready for foot traffic within 2–4 hours. We can also schedule cleaning so drying happens overnight before your staff arrives the next morning.
