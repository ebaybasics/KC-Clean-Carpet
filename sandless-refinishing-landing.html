The Problem with Traditional Refinishing

Traditional Sanding Is Expensive,
Messy, and Exhausting

If you've priced traditional hardwood floor refinishing before, you already know. Most Kansas City homeowners are shocked by the reality — and there's a better way.

1

Dust Everywhere

Traditional sanding generates enormous volumes of fine wood dust that infiltrates every room in your home — into vents, closets, cabinets, and electronics. Cleaning up after takes days longer than the refinishing itself.

2

Days of Disruption

Sanding, staining, and applying multiple finish coats takes 4–7 days minimum. Many families must relocate or live in a fraction of their home during the process.

3

High Cost

Full sand-and-refinish averages $3–$8 per square foot in the KC market. For a 1,200 sq ft floor, that's $3,600–$9,600 — just for floors that already exist in your home.

4

It Wears Away Your Wood

Every sanding removes approximately 1/16″ of wood. Solid hardwood floors can only be sanded a finite number of times before the wood is too thin to refinish. It's a resource you spend with each refinish.

Our Process

Five Steps. One Day.
Beautiful Floors.

1
Deep Clean

We clean every inch of the floor surface to remove dirt, wax, oils, and residue — ensuring the new finish bonds perfectly.

2
Screening

A professional buffer with fine abrasive screens lightly scuffs the existing finish — no wood removed, minimal dust.

3
Edge Detail

All edges, corners, and wall lines are hand-buffed to ensure complete, consistent coverage throughout every room.

4
Tack & Clean

The floor is vacuumed and tack-clothed to remove every particle before coating — because anything left under the finish stays visible.

5
Finish Coat

Professional-grade polyurethane is applied in your chosen sheen with a T-bar applicator for uniform thickness and streak-free coverage.

Is Sandless Refinishing Right for You?

We'll Always Be Honest
About What Your Floors Need

Sandless refinishing is the right solution for most dull, scuffed, or worn hardwood floors. Here's a quick checklist to help you understand where your floors fall.

✓ Great Candidate — Sandless Works

✓Floors look dull, hazy, or have lost their sheen
✓Light surface scratches and scuff marks from daily use
✓Traffic lanes that look faded compared to less-used areas
✓Finish feels sticky or looks uneven
✓You want to refresh the look without the cost of full refinishing
✓You've had the floors for 5+ years and they've never been professionally refinished

⚠ May Need Traditional Refinishing

⚠Deep gouges or scratches that penetrate through finish into wood
⚠Stains that have soaked into the wood grain itself
⚠Warped, cupped, or structurally damaged boards
⚠Floors with wax finish (not polyurethane)
⚠Laminate or LVP flooring (no real wood finish to key)

Not sure which category your floors fall into? We'll assess them for free and tell you exactly what we recommend — with no obligation.
