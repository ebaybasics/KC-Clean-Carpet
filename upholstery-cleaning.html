Professional Upholstery Cleaning in Kansas City

Your furniture sees as much daily use as your floors — maybe more. Sofas, sectionals, dining chairs, and recliners absorb body oils, pet hair, food spills, dust mites, and allergens over time. Regular vacuuming helps, but professional cleaning is the only way to truly remove what's embedded deep in the fabric fibers.

KC Clean Carpets's upholstery cleaning technicians are trained to identify fabric types, choose the right cleaning method, and deliver results that refresh your furniture and extend its life — all without over-wetting or causing shrinkage.

Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

  • Fabric identification — We inspect the cleaning code (W, S, WS, or X) and test a hidden area to confirm the safest cleaning method for your specific fabric.
  • Dry soil extraction — A specialized upholstery tool removes loose dry soils and pet hair from the surface and crevices.
  • Pre-treatment — Problem areas and stains are pre-treated with a fabric-appropriate cleaning agent.
  • Hot water extraction or dry cleaning — Depending on the fabric code, we use low-moisture hot water extraction or dry-solvent methods to deep-clean the fibers.
  • Rinse & extract — All cleaning solution is thoroughly rinsed and extracted, leaving no sticky residue behind.
  • Speed drying — Air movers are used to speed up drying time, minimizing disruption to your home.

What We Clean

  • Sofas and sectionals
  • Loveseats and recliners
  • Dining and accent chairs
  • Ottomans and footstools
  • Headboards and bed frames
  • Window seat cushions and bench pads
  • Auto and RV interiors (ask for details)

Fabric Types We Service

  • Microfiber and microsuede
  • Cotton, linen, and blends
  • Polyester and nylon
  • Velvet and chenille
  • Leather and faux leather (conditioning available)
  • Wool and natural fiber upholstery

Pet Hair & Odor Elimination

Pets love furniture — and unfortunately, so do the odors they leave behind. Our enzyme-based deodorizing treatments break down odor-causing compounds at a molecular level, eliminating pet smells rather than just covering them up. We can also apply a fabric protector to help repel future stains and soiling.
