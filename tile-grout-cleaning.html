Professional Tile & Grout Cleaning in Kansas City

Tile is one of the most durable flooring surfaces available — but grout is a different story. Grout is a porous material that acts like a sponge, soaking up dirt, grease, bacteria, and moisture over time. Regular mopping actually makes it worse by pushing dirty water into the grout lines, where it sits and stains.

At KC Clean Carpet, we use high-pressure steam cleaning systems with specialized rotary tile tools to blast out what's embedded deep in your grout lines — restoring that bright, clean look that mopping simply can't achieve. The difference is dramatic and immediately visible.

Our Tile & Grout Cleaning Process

  • Pre-inspection — We assess the tile type, grout condition, and any heavily soiled or damaged areas before we begin.
  • Pre-treatment — An alkaline cleaning solution is applied to the tile and grout to break down grease, soap scum, and embedded grime.
  • High-pressure steam extraction — Our rotary tile cleaning tool uses pressurized hot water to agitate and flush out soils from both tile surfaces and grout lines simultaneously.
  • Detail cleaning — Corners, edges, and hard-to-reach areas are cleaned by hand to ensure complete coverage.
  • Rinse & extraction — All cleaning solution and loosened soils are immediately extracted, leaving the surface clean and nearly dry.
  • Grout sealing (optional) — We apply a professional-grade grout sealer to protect lines from future staining and make routine maintenance much easier.

Surfaces We Clean

  • Ceramic and porcelain floor tile
  • Natural stone (marble, travertine, slate, granite)
  • Bathroom and shower tile walls
  • Kitchen backsplash tile
  • Entryway and mudroom tile
  • Commercial tile floors
  • Pool surrounds and outdoor pavers

Grout Sealing — Why It Matters

Freshly cleaned grout is at its most vulnerable — the pores are open and will begin absorbing soils again immediately. A professional-grade grout sealer creates a protective barrier that repels liquids and makes future staining much less likely. We recommend sealing immediately after cleaning for maximum protection. Our sealers are available in natural-look (penetrating) or color-enhancing formulas.

Grout Color Restoration

If your grout has stained so deeply that cleaning alone won't fully restore it, ask about our grout color restoration service. We apply a professional-grade color seal that permanently restores the grout to a uniform color of your choice — making your floors look brand new without the cost of re-tiling.
