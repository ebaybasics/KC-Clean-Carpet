About KC Clean Carpet

Family owned & operated — quality over volume, every time

Roland Smith Founder & Owner · KC Clean Carpet · Raytown, MO

A Family Business Built on Trust & Quality

KC Clean Carpet was founded on a simple belief: that every customer deserves the same level of care and attention that you'd give a family member's home. We are a family owned and operated company, and that means something to us — it shapes every decision we make and every job we take on.

We recognize the great trust you place in us when you invite us into your home. That trust is never taken for granted. Unlike large national franchises built on volume and speed, we deliberately keep our operation focused and personal. We don't run a high-volume business — we run a high-quality one.

When you call KC Clean Carpet, you're not getting a different crew each time. You're getting a dedicated team that takes pride in their work, shows up on time, and won't leave until you're completely satisfied.

"We put customers first — not because it's good for business, but because it's the right thing to do."
