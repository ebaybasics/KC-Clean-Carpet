Rental Machine vs. Professional: The Honest Comparison

Rental machines wash carpet with lukewarm water, modest suction, and detergent that largely stays in the fiber. The leftover residue attracts new dirt — which is why DIY-cleaned carpet often resoils faster than it did before.

Professional extraction costs more than a rental for obvious reasons, but the per-year math usually favors it: it actually removes the soil (instead of redistributing it), it extends carpet life by years, and it doesn't eat your Saturday.
